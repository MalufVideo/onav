NIVEA - More Than Protecting

National NIVEA campaign produced entirely in Virtual Production at On+Av studio, using Disguise servers and 2D plates to transport talent to paradise settings of sun, beach, and nature — without leaving the studio.

Virtual Production Disguise 2D Plates LED Volume HDR

The Campaign

"More Than Protecting: It's truly caring." NIVEA, the #1 sun protection brand in Brazil, launched a campaign celebrating the freedom to enjoy the sun with confidence — at the beach, in the water, or anywhere. The visual narrative needed to convey freshness, nature, and that summer feeling all year round.

The challenge was clear: create multiple outdoor environments — beaches, pools, sunny landscapes — with full control over lighting and weather conditions, something impossible in real locations where time and light change every minute.

Virtual Production at On+Av Studio

The production was 100% executed in our Virtual Production studio under the technical direction of Nelson Maluf. We used Disguise servers to manage the playback of high-resolution 2D plates — real footage of paradise locations projected onto the LED panels, enveloping the talent in photorealistic scenery.

The choice for 2D plates instead of 3D environments was strategic: for a sun protection campaign, the absolute realism of images captured on location was essential. The plates delivered texture, color, and atmosphere indistinguishable from traditional filming, while the studio ensured total control over light and visual continuity.

Technical Solution

  • Disguise Servers processing 2D plates in real-time with uncompressed playback
  • HDR LED Panels reproducing locations with color and brightness fidelity
  • Simulated Natural Lighting from the LED itself, integrating the talent into the scene with coherent reflections and light tones
  • Total Continuity Control — the same "golden hour" maintained throughout the entire filming day

Results

The campaign was a total success. Virtual production allowed filming all scenarios in a single studio day, eliminating travel, unpredictable weather conditions, and on-location production costs. The final result preserved all the visual quality of a major outdoor production, with the efficiency and control that only Virtual Production offers.

Another project that proves how Disguise technology, combined with On+Av team expertise, transforms the way major brands produce advertising content — delivering visual excellence with agility and precision.
