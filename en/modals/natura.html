Natura Luna Nuit with Sabrina Sato

Launch campaign for the Natura Luna Nuit fragrance with Sabrina Sato, produced in Virtual Production at On+Av studio. The presenter stars in the new chapter of Natura Luna, connecting intensity, courage, and female power.

Virtual Production Disguise LED Volume Advertising

The Campaign

Under the concept "Let your passion speak louder", Natura Luna Nuit expands the brand's portfolio by exploring the night as a space for expression and intensity. With the exclusive Natura Chypre, the most striking and sophisticated olfactory path in perfumery, Luna fragrances are irresistible and vibrate at the same intensity as you.

Known for her authenticity and for living her choices with courage and intention, Sabrina Sato genuinely translates the Natura Luna universe. Her trajectory, marked by the freedom to experiment and surrender to the new, makes her the natural representation of the Luna woman.

Virtual Production

The advertising campaign was produced using **Virtual Production** technology with LED panels and **Disguise** servers, allowing the creation of sophisticated and atmospheric scenarios that translate the nocturnal and enveloping essence of the fragrance — all with total control of lighting and setting, without the need for external locations.

Technical Solution

  • Disguise Servers managing visual content in real-time
  • HDR LED Panels creating immersive ambiance for the talent
  • Integrated lighting with natural LED reflections for visual coherence
  • Multiple scenarios created in a single studio day

About the Brand

"Natura Luna Nuit is born to celebrate the joy, spontaneity, and intensity of the Latin woman. The choice of Sabrina Sato reinforces this invitation to passion for life and to live one's own power with truth", says Tatiana Ponce, Vice President of Marketing and R&D at Natura.

With Natura Luna Nuit, Natura reinforces Natura Luna's positioning as a brand that values free choices, real stories, and the right of every woman to live her intensity in her own way. Intense like the night, her amber Chypre unites the enveloping warmth of amber with patchouli and the sweetness of red fruits.
