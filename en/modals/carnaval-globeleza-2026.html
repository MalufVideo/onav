We were invited by Alex Arrabal, who designed the artwork for this XR implementation, to join the technical team for Carnaval Globeleza 2026. With a long history of partnership with Globo, we were honored to make the technical aspects shine in an absolutely stunning studio environment.

AR / xR Disguise VX4 Disguise RXII Stype Tracking

The Challenge

To implement Extended Reality (XR) and Augmented Reality (AR) technical solutions in a high-profile live broadcast environment, ensuring tracking stability and precision to perfectly integrate virtual elements with the dazzling physical set.

Our Solution

Our team handled the entire calibration of the Stype tracking system and operated the media servers. We utilized our Disguise VX4 and RXII servers to drive the content, sending the live feed to the LED wall and compositing the Augmented Reality (AR) directly into the PGM (Program) feed.

Results

Everything was calibrated with extreme precision, resulting in a smooth operation during the live show. The integration between the real and virtual worlds worked amazingly well, delivering the visual quality demanded by an event of this magnitude.
