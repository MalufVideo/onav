Globo Agro 2025 — Beans

New film in the Globo Agro 2025 series, this time focused on bean culture. Produced in partnership with Rube and production company Piloto, using virtual production to bring the Brazilian countryside inside the studio.

Broadcast LED Walls Disguise TV Production

Overview

Another film in the Globo Agro 2025 campaign, this time dedicated to the **bean** culture. Like the other films in the series, it was made in partnership with Rube and production company Piloto, being broadcast nationwide. The project reaffirms how virtual production transforms complex logistics into an efficient workflow.

Technical Challenge

The technical challenge followed the same line as the other films in the series: offering total control over every element of the 3D scene in real-time. Parameters such as light position and intensity, scene elements, time of day, and subtle movements were exposed directly on set for the direction to adjust live.

The film used Unreal Engine integrated with camera tracking and Disguise servers, allowing 3D scenes in real-time on LED panels, as well as 2D plates combined with AI resources to maintain fine control over the final result.

Our Solution

We maintained the same production flow as the other films in the series, with the studio functioning as a "hub" for all locations. We were responsible for configuring, programming, and parametrizing the scenes so the director had total autonomy during filming.

We integrated the content into an Unreal Engine + Disguise + camera tracking pipeline, exposing controls for color, light, element position, and atmosphere directly on set. Adjustments that, on a real location, would require hours or days, could be made in minutes, live, with the client and direction monitoring on screen.

Results

Another film in the series with an impressive visual result, reinforcing the campaign's consistency. The scenario representing the bean culture maintains the same aesthetic and narrative coherence as the other films, preserving the identity of the portrayed culture.

This film further consolidates Globo Agro 2025 as one of our best cases of virtual production applied to agribusiness and broadcast, demonstrating in practice how it is possible to save time and resources without sacrificing quality and visual ambition.
