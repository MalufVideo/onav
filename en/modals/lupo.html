Lupo Father's Day

Pioneering virtual production in Brazil during the pandemic, using Unreal Engine vanilla nDisplay directly on 2.9mm LED panels.

Unreal Engine nDisplay LED 2.9mm Pioneering

Project Challenge

This project marks a historic moment: the early days of virtual production in Brazil during the pandemic, when this technology was little talked about and not as advanced as today. O2 Filmes called us to provide an innovative solution for Lupo's Father's Day campaign.

The challenge was to deliver real-time 3D rendering when this technology was practically unknown in the Brazilian market.

Our Pioneering Solution

On was already ahead of the times, seeking the best technology to deliver real-time rendering for cinema, series, and TV production.

Since this project happened before Disguise arrived in Brazil, we implemented an innovative solution:

  • Unreal Engine vanilla with nDisplay directly on 2.9mm LED panel
  • Real-time 3D rendering without intermediaries
  • Cutting-edge technology adapted for the Brazilian reality

Results

A super result for the time, establishing the foundations of virtual production in Brazil. This project demonstrated our early vision of the audiovisual future, positioning On as a pioneer in technologies that would only later become mainstream.

The Lupo campaign not only met its commercial objectives but also marked the beginning of a technological revolution in the Brazilian audiovisual production market.
