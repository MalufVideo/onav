Claro Flamingo Campaign | Anitta

Overview

In partnership with O2 Filmes, we participated in the "Claro Flamingo" campaign with Anitta, filmed in Rio de Janeiro. Even outside our studio, we brought our infrastructure to create a cinematic tropical set combining lifestyle, fashion, and pop technology.

Creative Challenge

It wouldn't be possible to take Anitta to a real beach due to schedule, security, and logistics issues. We needed to create a hyper-realistic beach environment with controlled climate, comfort for the crew, and zero weather risks, maintaining the dynamism of a movie set.

Our Solution

We set up a 9-meter straight LED panel loaded with photorealistic beach plates, operated with our Aximmetry servers ideal for compact volumes. We complemented with physical props, warm lighting, and controlled ventilation to deliver the tropical mood without leaving the set.

Results

Anitta recorded with total comfort and zero weather exposure, while production maintained creative control of every take. We delivered consistent images for the main film, social cuts, and photos, proving that the perfect beach can exist inside a climate-controlled set.
