[SUPER LIVE] The Millionaire Decade

Overview

In partnership with Fábula Live, we hosted "The Millionaire Decade", a super live stream lasting several hours, combining launches, mentorships, and live performances. The ambition was to build a continuous experience, with television language and absolute stability from the first to the last block.

Creative Challenge

It was the largest live stream we have ever executed: hours straight on air, thousands of simultaneous viewers, and no room for internet, power, or synchronization failures. Additionally, the client chose to use our original 16 x 5 meter curved LED volume, which required aligning the content displayed on the panel with the graphic identity appearing in the stream.

Our Solution

We configured the curved LED to act as an immersive portal, displaying only key moments while the bulk of information followed in GC and lower thirds in the stream. We set up redundant network backbones, dedicated uplink, and power failover, as well as a camera park and full-duplex intercom that allowed conducting the marathon with television-like fluidity.

Results

The broadcast stayed on air for hours without drops, with impeccable switching between LED content and the visual identity that went to the stream. The client celebrated the historic reach, audience retention, and the fact of having sustained the brand's largest live operation with premium broadcast quality.
