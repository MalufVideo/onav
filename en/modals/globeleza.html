Carnaval Globeleza 2026 — TV Globo

Technical implementation of Augmented Reality (AR) and Stype tracking calibration for the live broadcast of Carnaval Globeleza 2026 on TV Globo, using Disguise VX4 and RXII media servers to drive the LED wall and insert AR into the PGM output.

The Project

Carnaval Globeleza 2026 marked yet another edition of TV Globo's iconic carnival broadcast, and On+Av was invited to lead the technical implementation of Augmented Reality and xR in the studio. The invitation came through Alex Arrabal, who signed the artwork for this xR implementation. Alex has a long history with Globo and a deep understanding of how the network likes to approach visual aesthetics — the studio looked absolutely amazing.

We had the honor of making the entire technical side work: calibrating the Stype tracking system, configuring the Disguise VX4 and RXII servers, and integrating the live feed into the LED wall with AR insertion directly into the PGM (Program Output) of the broadcast.

Calibration & Tracking

One of the most critical aspects of the project was the Stype tracking system calibration. For Augmented Reality to work flawlessly during a live broadcast, the camera system needs to know exactly where it is in 3D space on every single frame. Our team calibrated the tracking with millimeter precision, ensuring that virtual elements integrated seamlessly with the real set throughout the entire broadcast.

The Disguise VX4 servers were responsible for processing content in real-time and feeding the LED wall with the artwork created by Alex Arrabal, while the Disguise RXII processed the AR layer that was inserted directly into the program signal (PGM), creating the illusion of 3D elements integrated into the real set for viewers at home.

Technical Implementation

The technical solution involved a complete real-time processing chain:

  • Disguise VX4 — primary server feeding the LED wall with real-time content, synchronized with camera tracking
  • Disguise RXII — processing the Augmented Reality layer inserted directly into the PGM
  • Stype Tracking — camera tracking system calibrated with precision for real-time 3D positioning
  • Broadcast integration — live LED wall feed + AR composited into the program signal for national broadcast
  • Art direction by Alex Arrabal — virtual sets created specifically for Globo's xR format
