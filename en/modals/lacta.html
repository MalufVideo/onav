Lacta - This Calls for a Lacta with Selton Mello

National campaign for Lacta starring Selton Mello, the brand's official ambassador, produced with Virtual Production technology using Disguise and Unreal Engine.

Virtual Production Disguise Unreal Engine Broadcast

The Campaign

The "This Calls for a Lacta" campaign invites consumers to recognize everyday moments that go well with the brand's chocolate – whether after lunch, during a series episode, or after an intense day. Created by David, the action runs until October in three phases: small achievements, coexistence challenges, and moments of pure pleasure.

The goal is to celebrate not only life's major milestones but also the underrated victories of daily life, like simply ending a busy day well.

Virtual Production at On+Av Studio

The production was carried out at On+Av studio using cutting-edge Virtual Production technology. Under the technical direction of Nelson Maluf, our team implemented a complete system with Disguise servers and Unreal Engine to create virtual environments in real-time.

The partnership with **Rubelabs**, production company **Mercúria**, and **Hogarth** allowed the creation of multiple virtual scenarios representing diverse Brazilian everyday moments. Virtual Production technology enabled Selton Mello to interact with digital environments in real-time, creating an authentic and engaging experience.

Technical Implementation

We used the Disguise platform integrated with Unreal Engine for real-time rendering of virtual environments. This configuration allowed instant adjustments to lighting, scenarios, and camera during filming, providing unprecedented creative flexibility and significantly reducing post-production time.

Broadcasting and Results

With national broadcasting, the campaign premiered on TV Globo during the breaks of the soap opera "Etá Mundo Melhor" and sponsoring episodes of "Que História é Essa, Porchat?". The 360º strategy also includes streaming (Globoplay, Netflix, and Prime Video), social media, digital media (Globo.com, iFood, and Google), as well as out-of-home and movie theaters.

Virtual Production technology allowed creating multiple scenarios with production efficiency, maintaining the high visual quality required for a large-scale national campaign. The result is modern and impactful communication that connects the Lacta brand with special moments of Brazilian daily life.
