New T-Cross | A SUVW Adventure | VW Brazil

Utilization of Disguise VX 4 servers and 100m² 2.6mm 10-bit HDR LED volume.

VX Series LED Volume HDR

Project Challenge

The project required implementing a large HDR LED panel and synchronizing multiple high-resolution tiles. The presence of celebrities and Volkswagen's security added complexity to the production, requiring a controlled and efficient environment.

Our Solution

We implemented an LED volume with 100m² of high-resolution panels (2.6mm pitch) with 10-bit HDR capability for vibrant and realistic colors. The robustness of the Disguise VX 4 servers ensured flawless production, allowing the creative team to focus on artistic aspects while our technical team ensured the virtual environment's perfect functioning.

Results

The technology allowed the commercial to be produced in just one day, with efficiency and high visual quality. The LED volume was not just a background for the car, but became a narrative element that contributed to telling the story of the New T-Cross as a versatile and adventurous vehicle, suitable for diverse environments and situations.
