Ludmilla at Coachella

We were hired by Ludmilla's creative team to program the show on Disguise for their performance at Coachella in the USA, which uses Disguise servers at the festival. We provided hardware for the rehearsal in Rio de Janeiro and programmed the show using monitors that simulated the Coachella LED panels.

Disguise Show Festival

Production Details

Our team was responsible for all visual programming of Ludmilla's show at Coachella, one of the world's biggest music festivals. We used Disguise servers to create an impressive visual experience that perfectly complemented the artist's energy.

To ensure perfect synchronization between performance and visuals, we held rehearsals in Rio de Janeiro using monitors to simulate the Coachella stage LED panels. This approach allowed us to refine every detail of the presentation before the big day.

The result was a visually stunning spectacle that highlighted Ludmilla's contagious energy on the main stage of Coachella, marking a striking presence at one of the most important events in world music.
