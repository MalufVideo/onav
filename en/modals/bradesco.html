Bradesco Insurance

Institutional campaign for Bradesco Insurance using state-of-the-art virtual production technology.

Virtual Production LED Volume Disguise Institutional Campaign

Overview

The **Bradesco Insurance** campaign is another classic case where **virtual production** truly shone. It was a huge pleasure working in partnership with the **Boiler** team, who arrived with the challenge of filming in three different scenarios on three distinct panels in a single shooting day.

For this, virtual production proved to be the ideal tool. We deployed our entire technology ecosystem: studio, LED panels, **Disguise** servers, and also our creative services team. Once again, we acted end-to-end – from the technical environment to visual development – to make a complex film viable within a very tight time window.

Technical Challenge

The great challenge of this project was time: we had one set day to film very different scenarios. Right in the first scene, we needed a flower field, something that on location would require enormous effort for setup, set dressing, and teardown. Our solution was to divide the project into three distinct LED configurations: a flower field in the LED volume, with light coming from all sides; a slow-motion scene with high-speed petals on our panel prepared for high speed; and finally, a completely red room created in **Unreal Engine**, aligned with the brand's colors.

Our Solution

For Bradesco Insurance, we acted as a true **one-stop shop**. In a single place, the client had access to the studio, technology, specialized technical team, and creative services to build the virtual environments. We operated with high-definition LED panels integrated with **Disguise** servers for real-time playback, developed the scenarios in **Unreal Engine** based on references brought by the direction, and, in the case of the flower field, used **generative AI** to efficiently create backgrounds while maintaining the visual quality required by the film.

Results

The final result is a visually sophisticated film that sensitively translates the brand's message. Actress **Bella Campos**, who came directly from a soap opera set in Rio de Janeiro to film in São Paulo, found an agile, organized, and creative work environment. The production company and direction worked with peace of mind, knowing they had flexibility for real-time adjustments, and we had the satisfaction of seeing another project where technology, art, and performance walk together. In the end, this case reinforces how virtual production is a powerful ally for campaigns that require multiple scenarios, little time, and high aesthetic standards – all within a single studio.
