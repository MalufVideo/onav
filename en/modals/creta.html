New Hyundai CRETA

Disguise VX4 servers with real-time uncompressed multi-video playback on 2.6mm HDR LED volume (180º array).

VX4 Series LED Volume HDR 180º Array

Overview

In partnership with Rube, the launch of the New Hyundai CRETA required an innovative approach to highlight the vehicle's design and features. We used our LED panels in a volume configuration to create an engaging visual experience that allowed presenting the vehicle from various angles and scenarios.

Technical Solution

We implemented an LED system with high-resolution 2.6mm panels in a volume configuration, creating an immersive environment for the vehicle. Disguise VX4 servers were used to manage real-time playback of multiple uncompressed videos, ensuring exceptional visual quality with HDR support for more vibrant and realistic colors.

Benefits

Using the Disguise system with LED panels allowed creating an impactful visual experience. The vehicle could be presented in various scenarios without the need for travel, saving time and production resources while maintaining the highest visual quality.
