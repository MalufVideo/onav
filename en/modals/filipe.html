Filipe Ret DVD

Main Description:

Supply of Disguise VX and RX servers for a bold and innovative project in partnership with LPL and Duo2 in the national concert scene.

Disguise VX and RX Notch Effects DVD Recording

Project Challenge

A super bold project that brought innovation to the Brazilian concert market: creating a revolutionary visual experience for Filipe Ret's DVD in Rio de Janeiro, combining a large amount of LED panels with real-time effects generated by Notch.

Our Participation

We provided Disguise VX4 and RXII servers to our partners LPL and Duo2, who were responsible for the complete execution of this spectacular production.

Our technology was specifically used to:

  • Process Notch effects in real-time
  • Playback extremely high-resolution and quality images on LED panels
  • Deliver visual effects synchronized with the performance

Results

The result was absolutely wonderful! LPL and Duo2 created a show that drove the audience wild, utilizing the maximum technology available. The DVD became a milestone in the national music scene, demonstrating how the partnership between cutting-edge technology and creative expertise can elevate music productions to a new level.

The complete spectacle can be watched directly on YouTube, recording this special moment of Brazilian music forever.
