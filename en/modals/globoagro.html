Globo Agro 2025

Series of films for Globo Agro 2025 produced in partnership with Rube and production company Piloto, using virtual production to bring multiple regions of Brazil inside the studio.

Broadcast LED Walls Disguise TV Production

Overview

The Globo Agro 2025 campaign was another project realized in partnership with Rube and production company Piloto, a series of films broadcast nationwide. It is a perfect case of how virtual production transforms complex logistics into an efficient workflow.

Each film in the series addresses a different agricultural culture, located in distinct regions of Brazil. In a traditional production model, this would mean weeks of travel across the country, with high costs for travel, crew, and infrastructure. With virtual production, we managed to bring all these locations inside the studio and, in two or three days of shooting, film multiple scenarios representing different parts of Brazil with visual consistency and creative freedom.

Technical Challenge

From a technical point of view, the main challenge was meeting a very specific demand from the direction: total control over every element of the 3D scene. To achieve this, we structured the project to expose parameters in real-time on set.

In each environment, the director could adjust light position and intensity, scene element positions, time of day, and even the speed of subtle movements, like a fan turning on the wall. A large part of the films was made with Unreal Engine integrated with camera tracking and Disguise servers, allowing 3D scenes in real-time. In other segments, we used 2D plates combined with AI resources, always maintaining fine control over the final result.

Our Solution

The solution was to design a production flow where the studio functioned as a "hub" for all project locations. Even though we didn't create the original art for all scenarios, we were responsible for configuring, programming, and parametrizing the scenes so the director had total autonomy during filming.

We integrated the content into an Unreal Engine + Disguise + camera tracking pipeline, exposing controls for color, light, element position, and atmosphere directly on set. Adjustments that, on a real location, would require hours or days, could be made in minutes, live, with the client and direction monitoring on screen.

Results

The visual result of the campaign is impressive: at many moments, it is difficult to tell where the real ends and the virtual begins. The scenarios representing different regions of Brazil maintain aesthetic and narrative coherence while preserving the identity of each culture portrayed.

From a production standpoint, the gain is evident: instead of weeks on the road, we concentrated everything in a few studio days, with greater control, safety, and predictability. The LED panels, camera tracking, and the entire virtual production infrastructure responded with precision, allowing complex shots without compromising the schedule. This project consolidated itself as one of our best cases of virtual production applied to agribusiness and broadcast, showing in practice how it is possible to save time and resources without sacrificing quality and visual ambition.
