Primo Rico Ultra Black Friday Infinite [LIVE]

Overview

In partnership with LOC7 and Grupo Primo, we transformed our film studio into a hybrid environment of live show, concert, and TV program to host the main digital marketing influencers during the Ultra Black Friday. The briefing required stage presence, television pacing, and total redundancy for a massive audience connected in real-time.

Creative Challenge

The live stream needed to deliver a hybrid experience mixing live show and premium talk show, maintaining visual clarity for both those on set and millions of online viewers. Additionally, the client demanded deep integration with remote participation platforms and an atypical number of cameras to ensure dynamic cuts, even with presenters switching between different segments.

Our Solution

We designed a volume with a straight LED panel that functioned as an interactive canvas, perfect for graphics, rankings, and insertions in real-time. We operated half a dozen static cameras, long lenses, gimbal rigs, and a camMate to give television language. Disguise servers programmed via API synchronized with the switcher and allowed the Grupo Primo team to trigger content with autonomy.

Results

We integrated hundreds of simultaneous participations via Zoom directly on the panel, without compromising latency or aesthetics. The broadcast sustained hours of live content with stability, raised audience engagement, and delivered the client's main objective: revenue record in a single Black Friday night.
