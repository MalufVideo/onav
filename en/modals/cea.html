LED HDR Virtual Production Disguise Fashion Campaign

Project Challenge

C&A wanted an advertising campaign reflecting its innovative spirit in the fashion market. The challenge was to create multiple stunning visual environments in a single studio, with Adriana Lima as the protagonist, transitioning between scenarios ranging from a futuristic metropolis to an elegant golden room.

Our Solution

In partnership with Rube, we implemented large-format HDR LED panels with Disguise servers for real-time virtual production. The technology allowed creating environments impossible with traditional methods, such as runway shows with vibrant urban horizons in the background.

Results

The resulting commercial raised the standard for advertising productions in the fashion market, demonstrating how virtual production optimizes processes by eliminating travel between locations, while infinitely expanding creative possibilities.

The campaign perfectly reflected C&A's innovative spirit, creating a visually stunning universe that impactfully highlighted both the brand and its ambassador.
