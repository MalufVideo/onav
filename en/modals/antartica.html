Project Challenge

Globo brought us a fascinating challenge: bring freezing Antarctica into Rio de Janeiro at 40 degrees. How to transform a Rio studio into a convincing polar landscape for the pilot of the Antarctica series? This was one of the most stimulating projects we have ever faced.

Our Solution

We implemented a complete virtual production ecosystem:

  • 100m² of 2.6mm HDR LED panels for the main volume
  • 5000 nits LED ceiling for natural polar lighting
  • Disguise VX4 and RXII servers for real-time rendering
  • RedSpy tracking system for precise camera movement
  • Disguise technical expertise for perfect integration of all elements

Results

The combination of our technology with Globo's fantastic art department, an incredible practical effects team, and beautiful cinematography work created an extremely photorealistic setting.

The result was so convincing that it became impossible to distinguish that the film was made in Rio de Janeiro and not in the ice of Antarctica. The magic of virtual production completely transformed reality, proving that with the right technology, any environment can be recreated with cinematic perfection.
