Globo Promo Day

xR powered by Disguise for experiences with LED wall and LED floor immersing talent in the 3D virtual environment.

xR LED Floor Virtual Production Broadcast

The Project

Globo Promo Day was an innovative pre-Olympics event that used Extended Reality (xR) technology to create an immersive virtual environment for the broadcaster's presenters and talents. The challenge was to create a space where presenters could appear to be in Paris or in virtual environments created by Globo's art department.

Technical Implementation

We used the Disguise platform with 5 servers to create a complete xR environment, combining vertical LED panels with an LED floor. This configuration allowed creating a virtual volume where presenters could move freely on the LED floor and with 3D virtual elements generated in real-time.

The camera tracking system integrated with Disguise allowed physical camera movements to translate perfectly into the virtual environment, creating a cohesive visual and parallax experience for viewers.

Results

The use of xR technology allowed Globo to create visually impactful promotions with complex virtual environments that would be impossible to film in the time they had to go on air. The ability to instantly change scenarios brought creative flexibility and operational efficiency, resulting in high-quality content with less production time.
