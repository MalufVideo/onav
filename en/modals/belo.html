BELO, TOO CLOSE TO THE LIGHT

Supply of Disguise VX4 and RXII servers for the documentary about singer Belo's 50-year career at Surreal studio.

Disguise VX4 RXII Documentary Unreal Engine Surreal Studio Musical Biography

Project Challenge

Our partners Alexandre Arrabal and Césio Lima from Surreal studio invited us for a special project: the documentary "Too Close to the Light", which portrays singer Belo's 50-year career.

The challenge was to create visual environments that complemented a complex narrative, covering various controversies of the artist's career, celebrity testimonials, and iconic moments of his musical trajectory.

Our Role

We provided Disguise VX4 and RXII servers to render Unreal Engine projects in real-time on Surreal studio's LED panels.

Our technology was specifically used for:

  • Transition moments between testimonials and historical archives
  • Dynamic scenarios contextualizing different career phases
  • Immersive environments transporting viewers through 5 decades of Brazilian music

Results

The partnership with Surreal resulted in a visually engaging documentary that honored Belo's complex trajectory. Disguise servers allowed fluid transitions between reality and memory, creating a visual narrative that amplified the emotional impact of the testimonials.

The project demonstrates how virtual production can elevate biographical documentaries, transforming conventional interviews into immersive cinematic experiences.
