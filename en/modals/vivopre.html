Overview

The **Vivo Pré** campaign was filmed in an external studio with its own infrastructure of LED panels and virtual production technology. On+Av's participation in this project focused on **content creation** — we developed the images and backgrounds displayed on the panels during filming.

Our Participation

In this case, On+Av acted exclusively in **creating visual content and backgrounds** used in virtual production. The entire studio, LED panels, and technical infrastructure were provided by third parties. Our work focused on developing the virtual environments that served as scenarios for the commercials, ensuring the backgrounds were aligned with the art direction and the campaign's visual identity.

Result

The Vivo Pré commercials demonstrate the quality and versatility of content created by On+Av for virtual production. Even without providing the LED and studio infrastructure, the final result reflects our capacity to create immersive and visually impactful virtual environments that meet the requirements of major brands and advertising campaigns of national reach.
