Avon advertising campaign with Ana Castela using cutting-edge virtual production technology.

Virtual Production LED Volume Disguise Beauty Campaign

Overview

The Avon with Ana Castela campaign was another project in partnership with Rube that shows, in practice, the power of virtual production. The creative proposal called for the artist in a North American desert setting, with mountains and wide horizons. Instead of traveling abroad, we brought the desert inside a studio in São Paulo, using our LED volume, Disguise servers, content in Unreal Engine, and Stype camera tracking to recreate, in real-time, the landscapes of Utah/Texas.

Creative Challenge

Besides providing the technology and studio, we were responsible for conceiving and producing the entire 3D environment of the film. The director arrived with references and static images of the type of desert she imagined, and our challenge was to transform this moodboard into a credible and cinematic virtual scenario. We created the desert in Unreal Engine, mountains, and atmosphere until we reached exactly the desired look. One of the most complex segments involved a car at high speed crossing the desert, requiring the render node to sustain rapid movement without loss of quality.

Complete sequence used in the LED volume showing the car at high speed through the desert.

Our Solution

Our delivery was a complete virtual production package. We used our state-of-the-art studio in São Paulo, equipped with a 16 x 5 meter HDR LED volume, 2.6 mm pitch, operating with RGB 4:4:4 signal without compression in real-time. The 3D content ran on a Disguise RXII server, ensuring performance and color fidelity, while the Stype Red Spy tracking system ensured perfect parallax between camera, scenery, and artist movement, giving the direction total freedom to explore angles, compositions, and light variations.

Results

The visual result was impeccable and exceeded everyone's expectations. Ana Castela felt at ease in a scenario that responded like a real environment, Avon approved the film with enthusiasm, and the production company worked with confidence, knowing they had a flexible system for real-time adjustments. For us, the biggest return was seeing a collaborative and creative set, where technology and storytelling walked together. With this campaign, we reinforce our commitment to delivering technical and creative excellence, showing how virtual production transforms ambitious ideas into concrete images without leaving the studio.
