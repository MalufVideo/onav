O Boticário

Advertising campaign for O Boticário produced with LED panels and technical team from On+Av. The studio and content creation were handled by production partners.

Advertising LED Walls Virtual Production Technical Team

Overview

The O Boticário campaign was a project where On+Av participated by providing LED panels and the specialized technical team for on-set operation. The studio and visual content creation were the responsibility of our production partners.

Our Role

In this project, On+Av was responsible for supplying and operating the LED panels used as virtual production backgrounds, as well as the entire technical team needed for assembly, calibration, and equipment operation during filming. The content displayed on the panels and the studio where production took place were provided by partners.

Result

The result is a commercial with impeccable visual quality, where LED panels created immersive and realistic scenarios. The integration between On+Av's infrastructure and the partners' creative content demonstrates the versatility of our operating model, which adapts to each project's needs — whether as a complete solution or as a specialized technology and team provider.
