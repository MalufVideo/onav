FIFAe Finals 2025 — Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Technical operation with 5 Disguise VX4 servers powering over 20 LED panels at the planet's largest esports event — the FIFAe Finals 2025, at SEF Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

Disguise VX4 ×5 20+ LED Walls Live Event Broadcast Esports

The Event

The FIFAe Finals 2025 gathered the world's best esports players in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. The tournament featured multiple titles — Rocket League and eFootball — in a global-scale competition broadcast to millions of viewers worldwide.

The stage? The SEF Arena, one of the planet's largest and most advanced esports arenas, with 25,000m² of total area, 11,000m² of flexible stages, and capacity for over 2,200 spectators in the main hall. A space worthy of the largest esports competition in the FIFA ecosystem.

Project Challenge

The event's scale was colossal: multiple stages, five halls dedicated to different tournaments, live broadcast to a global audience, and the requirement for perfect synchronization between visual content, real-time gameplay, and broadcasting. Any technical failure would be visible to millions of viewers around the world.

The challenge: manage over 20 LED panels in multiple configurations, with dynamic content following each match in real-time — all in a week-long event with no margin for error.

Technical Solution

On+Av was responsible for operating the Disguise servers that powered the entire event's visual infrastructure. Our technical team implemented and operated a robust and redundant system, designed to support a whole week of live competition without interruptions.

  • 5 Disguise VX4 servers operating simultaneously to manage all event visual content
  • Over 20 LED panels distributed across stages, backstage, and broadcast areas
  • Real-time dynamic content synchronized with gameplay and live broadcast
  • Continuous operation for 8 days with 24/7 technical support
  • Total redundancy — no downtime seconds during the entire competition

Results

The operation was an absolute success. During the live competition, the Disguise servers operated by On+Av maintained 100% uptime, powering over 20 LED surfaces with real-time dynamic content for a global audience of millions.

This project consolidated On+Av's presence in the international live events scene, demonstrating our ability to operate on a global scale with the technical excellence the Disguise ecosystem demands. From SEF Arena in Riyadh to the world — On+Av delivers, no matter the challenge size.
